Route 02 · Closed Offer

Who this route is for.

Later-stage private companies: VC-backed and pre-IPO businesses that want a regulated valuation point, and room for early backers to realise value, before the main market.

Indicative profile: a valuation of AED 1.5B or more, raising AED 375M or more. Confirmed together on your first call.

Eligibility, in brief.

The core requirements still apply, with a profile suited to a Closed Offer.

  • Revenue-generating for 2 financial years
  • UAE-incorporated, onshore or Free Zone
  • Private Joint Stock Company or PLC
  • Indicative valuation of AED 1.5B or more
  • Raising AED 375M or more
  • Offer limited to approved investors, no public offer
Run the eligibility check

Indicative only. Everything is confirmed with DFM on your first call.

How the raise works.

A Closed Offer to invited investors, typically 4 to 8 weeks from first call to allocation.

  1. Step 01

    Discovery

    First conversation and eligibility review, in confidence.

  2. Step 02

    Preparation

    The offer terms and disclosure pack take shape with your adviser.

  3. Step 03

    Invitation round

    Approved investors receive the offer and place their orders.

  4. The milestone

    Allocation

    Shares are allocated and admitted, and change hands in private trading windows.
