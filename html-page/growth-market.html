Who is this designed for?

This option is for established companies that want to raise money for growth by offering shares to investors through DFM. You decide how much to raise and how much of your company to offer, and our team guides you from the first call to listing day. The process takes about 3 to 4 months, and from then on your shares can be bought and sold on a regulated market.

Indicative profile: a valuation of around AED 300 to 500M, raising AED 75 to 125M. Confirmed together on your first call.

How the raise works.

From your first call to listing day in about 3 to 4 months.

  1. 2 weeks

    Discovery

    A first conversation and a check that your company qualifies, followed by an invitation to apply.

  2. 6 to 8 weeks

    Preparation

    Your DFM relationship manager and your financial adviser come on board. Together you prepare the prospectus, the document investors read before they decide.

  3. 3 to 5 weeks

    Roadshow

    You meet investors, hear how much interest there is and arrive at an indicative price.

  4. 2 to 3 weeks

    Subscription

    The offer opens and investors apply for shares online.

  5. The milestone

    Listing day

    Shares are allocated to investors and start trading on a regulated market.

Eligibility, in brief.

The seven core requirements. The eligibility check covers the same seven.

  • Revenue-generating for 2 financial years
  • UAE-incorporated, onshore or Free Zone
  • Private Joint Stock Company or PLC
  • Fully paid-up share capital
  • Minimum AED 5M paid-up capital (USD 200k for PLC)
  • At least 25% of shares offered to investors, or a raise of AED 35M, whichever is lower
  • Shares unencumbered, equal rights to owners

Who your investors will be.

Your offer is open to everyone with a DFM investor number: individuals, and institutions such as funds and banks. That is more than a million investor accounts.

How they hear about your offer

Your offer is announced on ARENA and appears on its offerings board. Before it opens, you meet investors on a roadshow and DFM runs investor education about your company.

How they invest

During the subscription period, investors apply for shares online in the iVestor app or through their broker.

How they stay with you

From listing day they are your shareholders. They can buy and sell your shares on DFM, and they follow your progress through your results and announcements.
