Route 01 · Open Offer

Who this route is for.

Established private companies ready to raise from the wide market: SMEs and growth companies with audited financials, a working board and a growth plan to fund.

Indicative profile: a valuation around AED 300–500M, raising AED 75–125M. Confirmed together on your first call.

Eligibility, in brief.

The core requirements for an Open Offer on the Growth Market, the same seven checks as the eligibility tool.

  • Revenue-generating for 2 financial years
  • UAE-incorporated, onshore or Free Zone
  • Private Joint Stock Company or PLC
  • Fully paid-up share capital
  • Minimum AED 5mn paid-up capital (USD 200k for PLC)
  • Minimum 25% free float or AED 35mn raise (whichever is lower)
  • Shares unencumbered, equal rights to owners
Run the eligibility check

Indicative only. Our team can discuss your suitability and guide you through the applicable requirements.

How the raise works.

An Open Offer on DFM infrastructure, from first call to listing day in about 3 to 4 months.

  1. 2 weeks

    Discovery

    First conversation and eligibility review, then an invitation to apply.

  2. 6–8 weeks

    Preparation

    Your relationship manager and adviser come on board, and the prospectus takes shape.

  3. 3–5 weeks

    Roadshow

    Investor meetings test market appetite and set indicative pricing.

  4. 2–3 weeks

    Subscription

    The offer opens and investors subscribe digitally on DFM infrastructure.

  5. The milestone

    Listing day

    Allocation, admission and go-live. Your shares trading on a regulated market.
