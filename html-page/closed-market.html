Route 03 · Closed Listing

Who this route is for.

Family businesses and private companies that want ownership to move between generations and employees in a regulated, private setting.

No public offer and no minimum raise. The shape of the admission is agreed with the family on your first call.

Eligibility, in brief.

A lighter set of requirements, with no public offer at any stage.

  • UAE-incorporated family business or private company
  • Private Joint Stock Company or PLC
  • An independent valuation the family accepts
  • Ownership limited to an approved circle
  • Shares unencumbered, equal rights to owners
Run the eligibility check

Indicative only. Everything is confirmed with DFM on your first call.

How the raise works.

A Closed Listing with no public offer, typically 4 to 8 weeks from first call to admission.

  1. Step 01

    Discovery

    First conversation with the family, in confidence.

  2. Step 02

    Valuation

    An independent valuation sets a fair price for every member.

  3. Step 03

    Admission

    Family and employees subscribe through the company’s offer.

  4. The milestone

    A private market

    Ownership moves inside the approved circle, at a clear price.
