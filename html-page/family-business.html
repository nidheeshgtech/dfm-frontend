Who is this designed for?

This option is for family businesses that want to pass ownership to the next generation in an orderly and private way. An independent valuation sets a fair price, and shares can move only within a circle of owners that you approve, so nothing is offered to the public. It typically takes 4 to 8 weeks from your first call.

No public offer and no minimum raise. The details are agreed with the family on your first call.

A lighter set of requirements, with no public offer at any stage.

  • Private Joint Stock Company or PLC
  • An independent valuation the family accepts
  • Ownership limited to an approved circle
  • Shares unencumbered, equal rights to owners

Who can hold your shares.

There are no outside investors on this option. Shares are held by family members and the owners you approve.

Who is in the circle

You set the approved circle. Only the people in it can hold or buy shares.

How shares change hands

Members of the circle buy and sell between themselves through ARENA. Nothing appears on a public screen.

How they stay informed

You report to the approved circle only, not to the public.
