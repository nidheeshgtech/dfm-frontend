What is ARENA

Raising capital today can be complex, costly, and time-consuming. Arena simplifies the process. Arena connects companies with qualified investors, giving businesses a faster, more efficient way to access funding through a trusted platform powered by Dubai Financial Market.

01 — SMEs and growth companies

Private Market

Closed Market

Move shares privately, without going public.

Three routes to capital

Choose the route that fits your stage and your investors. All three run on DFM regulated platform for subscription, listing, trading, clearing and custody.
