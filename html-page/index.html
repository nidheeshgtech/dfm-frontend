Most asked questions.

Read the quick answers below, or ask the ARENA assistant directly.

ARENA is DFM’s planned private markets platform for growth capital and private shareholder liquidity. It serves operating growth businesses, pre-IPO companies and family businesses through three routes: Growth Market, Private Market and Closed Listing. ARENA is subject to approval by the Capital Market Authority (CMA).

The Main Market suits established, scaled businesses ready for public ownership — typically US$1bn+ equity value. ARENA is built for earlier-stage and private companies, with illustrative valuations of around US$95mn to US$820mn. It offers a faster, lower-cost route to market under a lighter-weight yet robust framework, with control over your cap table and privacy rails for financial disclosures.

No. ARENA is DFM’s planned regulated private market platform, designed to complement the Main Market by giving companies an alternative route to access capital and secondary liquidity. ARENA will operate within DFM’s existing market infrastructure, under the regulatory oversight of the UAE Capital Market Authority (CMA).

ARENA and Nasdaq Dubai are complementary platforms within DFM’s broader capital markets offering. ARENA is DFM’s planned regulated private market platform. Nasdaq Dubai is Dubai’s international exchange in the DIFC, offering equities, bonds, sukuk and other securities. Nasdaq Dubai is regulated by the Dubai Financial Services Authority (DFSA); ARENA will operate under the oversight of the UAE Capital Market Authority (CMA). Together, they give companies and investors access to a broader range of capital raising and investment opportunities.

Three authorities, one streamlined framework:

  • Capital Market Authority (CMA) — the UAE federal securities regulator (formerly SCA). Sets the offering rules, approves prospectuses, sets disclosure standards and licenses brokers and advisers.
  • Dubai Financial Market (DFM) — sets the ARENA listing rules and supervises the market.
  • Ministry of Economy and Free Zones — the company registrar. Sets the corporate governance code.

ARENA is expected to launch soon, subject to final regulatory approvals. The CMA has completed its public consultation on ARENA’s offering rules, and preparations are progressing towards launch. Register your interest to receive updates and be among the first to know when ARENA opens.

ARENA brings investment opportunities to global investors. DFM has 1.25 million+ registered investors from 200+ nationalities, both institutional and retail. Which companies you can invest in depends on the route each company uses (see I3).

Register on the ARENA platform. You can subscribe to offers via the platform and through brokers, and trade after listing through existing brokers.

It depends on the route:

  • Growth Market: open to institutional and registered retail investors.
  • Private Market: open to institutional investors, professionals and HNWI who are whitelisted by the company.
  • Closed Listing: limited to family members, employees and other permissioned investors. Not open to outside investors.

Private Market offers are available to permissioned, whitelisted investors only. Request access through the platform; the company decides whether to approve you.

Yes. ARENA is designed for global institutional and retail investors registered on the platform. On the Private Market route, the company can whitelist international investors.

ARENA companies have annual and semi-annual financial reporting and material disclosure duties. Growth Market companies report to ARENA investors through real-time market announcements. Private Market and Closed Listing companies report to whitelisted investors only. Investors can engage directly with company management and track company performance.

  • Growth Market: an open, continuous market, through existing brokers.
  • Private Market: closed RFQ (request for quote) trading and ad-hoc private auctions, for whitelisted investors only.
  • Closed Listing: closed RFQ and internal auctions, for family members and employees.

Growth Market shares trade daily. On the Private Market and Closed Listing routes, trading happens through RFQ and ad-hoc auctions, so it is less frequent and you may not be able to sell when you want.

Yes. A 180-day lock-up period applies to relevant shareholders, including founders and insiders, after an offering on ARENA. During this period, restricted shareholders cannot sell their shares, subject to applicable regulatory requirements and any permitted exemptions.

ARENA companies are growth-stage and private businesses, so their shares can carry higher risk than established listed companies. Trading on the Private Market and Closed Listing routes is less frequent. ARENA is subject to regulatory approval and its rules may change. Read each company’s prospectus or offer documents before investing.
