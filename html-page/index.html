Open offerings are available to investors with a DFM investor number, including qualified individuals and institutions. Private and Closed Market offerings are limited to approved investor circles.

Create an ARENA account, choose an open offering, pick your investor category, enter your amount and pay from your linked account. When the offer closes you are notified of your allocation.

If an offer is oversubscribed you may receive a partial allocation or none. Any unallocated amount is refunded to your account automatically.

Each offering sets its own minimum subscription. You see it on the offer page in iVestor before you commit to anything.

Any fees that apply to an offering are set out in its offer document, so you see the full picture before you subscribe.
