Who is this designed for?

This option is for companies whose employees own shares in the business through an employee share ownership plan, known as an ESOP. An independent valuation gives the shares a fair price, and employees can sell them to approved buyers without the company going public. It typically takes 4 to 8 weeks to set up.

How it works.

  1. Step 01

    Discovery

    A first conversation about your share plan, in confidence.

  2. Step 02

    Valuation

    An independent valuation sets a fair price for the shares.

  3. Step 03

    Admission

    Your shares are admitted to ARENA, with trading limited to employees and approved buyers.

  4. The milestone

    A way to sell

    Employees can sell their shares at a clear price, inside the approved circle.

Eligibility, in brief.

A lighter set of requirements, with no public offer at any stage.

  • UAE-incorporated family business or private company
  • Private Joint Stock Company or PLC
  • An independent valuation the family accepts
  • Ownership limited to an approved circle
  • Shares unencumbered, equal rights to owners
